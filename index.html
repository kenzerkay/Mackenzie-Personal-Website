About Me

Hi! I'm Mackenzie Ticoras, a fourth year dual-PhD student in Astrophysics and Computational Math, Science, and Engineering at Michigan State University and a National Science Foundation Graduate Research Fellow. My research focuses on investigating the spatial and kinematic structure of the ionized plasma surrounding galaxies, known as the circumgalactic medium (CGM). I use the high resolution FOGGIE simulations to investigate the distribution of hot and cold gas in the CGM. I am particularly interested in understanding how different components of absorption lines connect to the physical cloud structures in the CGM. Additionally, I am currently working on a set of isolated galaxy simulations to investigate the impact of magnetic fields and cosmic rays on absorption features that appear in mock observations. Outside of research, I am passionate about science communication, mentorship, and creating effective and equitable classrooms.
